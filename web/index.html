What am I looking at?

The opening collection is a dated selection of real museum records. Search queries both museums live and can be paged; the loaded-result filters act only on records already fetched. Different catalogues describe objects differently, and search ranking is supplied by each museum.

Dates, culture and attribution

Museum date labels are preserved, including approximate dates and ranges. The timeline groups range midpoints; year filters use overlapping ranges. A culture or place is the catalogue's description, not an inference about the artist. Undated works appear in the gallery but not the timeline.

Images, rights and credits

Images are displayed only when the source explicitly flags the artwork as public domain. Other records remain available as metadata with a museum link. Images load from the museums; credits and source links accompany every work. Check the current museum record before reusing an image.

Privacy and source access

Search sends your terms to the museum API. Images are requested from museum image servers. This tool does not save searches or comparisons between visits, and exported files are created in your browser. A museum can throttle requests or be temporarily unavailable; successful results from the other museum remain usable. Opening records identify whether they came from the live museum API or a museum-published data file; a file retrieval date does not imply current catalogue metadata.
