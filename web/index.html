What am I looking at?

The opening collection is a dated selection of real museum records. Live search queries the Met, Chicago and Cleveland and can be paged. NGA and Getty are represented by configured captured records, which are searchable with Refine loaded works; the loaded-result filters act only on records already fetched. Different catalogues describe objects differently, and search ranking is supplied by each museum.

Dates, culture and attribution

Museum date labels are preserved, including approximate dates and ranges. The timeline groups range midpoints; year filters use overlapping ranges. A culture or place is the catalogue's description, not an inference about the artist. Undated works appear in the gallery but not the timeline.

Images, rights and credits

Images are displayed only when the source explicitly flags them as public domain, CC0 or NGA Open Access. Other records remain available as metadata with a museum link. Images load from the museums; credits and source links accompany every work. Check the current museum record before reusing an image.

Print estimates use the linked image file’s pixel dimensions. Captured additions verify JPEG headers; live Cleveland results use dimensions supplied by the museum, never the physical size of the artwork or the gallery thumbnail. 300 PPI is a close-viewing estimate; 150 PPI is a larger-print estimate. These are uncropped dimensions, without margins or promises of print quality. A retrieval date is not a new catalogue revision.

Privacy and source access

Search sends your terms to the museum API. Images are requested from museum image servers. This tool does not save searches or comparisons between visits, and exported files are created in your browser. A museum can throttle requests or be temporarily unavailable; successful results from the other museum remain usable. Opening records identify whether they came from the live museum API or a museum-published data file; a file retrieval date does not imply current catalogue metadata.
